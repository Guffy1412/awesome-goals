### Learning & Inspiration

* [Google re:Work – Set Goals with OKRs](https://rework.withgoogle.com/guides/set-goals-with-okrs/) - Google's practical guide explains the Objectives and Key Results (OKR) framework with real-world examples and implementation advice.
* [MindTools – SMART Goals](https://www.mindtools.com/a4wo118/smart-goals) - A clear introduction to creating Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound goals.
* [James Clear](https://jamesclear.com/) - Offers evidence-based articles on habits, identity, and systems that support long-term goal achievement.
* [Michael Hyatt – Goal Setting](https://fullfocus.co/blog/category/goal-setting/) - Provides actionable articles and frameworks for setting, prioritizing, and achieving personal and professional goals.
* [Todoist Inspiration – Goals](https://todoist.com/inspiration) - Features productivity and goal-setting guides that help turn large objectives into manageable tasks.

### Goal Tracking & Planning Tools

* [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) - A flexible workspace with goal trackers, habit systems, and hundreds of customizable planning templates.
* [ClickUp Goals](https://clickup.com/features/goals) - Enables individuals and teams to set measurable goals, track progress, and connect objectives to daily work.
* [Goalscape](https://www.goalscape.com/) - Uses a visual radial map to organize, prioritize, and monitor complex goals effectively.
* [Lifetick](https://www.lifetick.com/) - A dedicated goal-setting platform built around SMART goals with progress tracking and accountability features.
* [Trello](https://trello.com/) - A simple Kanban-style project management tool that can be adapted for personal goal planning and milestone tracking.
